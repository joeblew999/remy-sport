# Plan — meetings: anyone, with anyone, notified

Status: **built and shipped**; recorded 2026-09-10 by reading the tree, because
this line still said "proposed" while the feature was live in production.

All six steps are done; step 6 carries one named gap. Evidence beside each box below —
none of it was written for this update; it is what is already there.

The Product Owner: *"the Meeting thing using MoQ needs to allow anyone to make a
meeting with one or many users. You might need to adjust the domain model. You
can decide it. The users should get a notification too via their chosen
notification option for that. We will not have any restrictions on it, because a
user / users can decide if they want to accept or not. You decide where you want
to place this into the App too, and what GUI is needed."*

Decisions are taken below rather than deferred, as asked.

## What exists

`moq.meetingConfig` is a **dev-only two-seat test** and says so in its own
policy: *"authenticated dev-only Hang media experiment; no meeting domain grants
or game state"*. Seats are the literals `a` and `b`, the room is a random hex
string, and it 404s outside `dev`. `pages/meeting-test.tsx` drives it. There is
no meeting in the domain, nothing is stored, and nobody is told.

So this is not an extension of that — it is the feature that test was proving
the media for. The test keeps working; the room reuses its media path.

## The decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who may create one? | **Anyone signed in.** `CREATE_MEETING` granted to `ANY_SIGNED_IN`. | The Product Owner's instruction. The protection is the invitation, not the gate: nothing happens to you until you accept. |
| Who may be invited? | **Any account.** A new `people.list` returns id and name. | You cannot invite people you cannot name. It returns no address and no role — a name and an id, which is what a picker needs. |
| Reuse `INVITATION`, or a new type? | **New: `MEETING_INVITE`,** in a new `MEETING` category. | `INVITATION` is the co-organiser invite and its copy says so. The matrix groups by category, so a new one gets its own group rather than sitting under Live. |
| Push only, or email too? | **Both**, so a `meetingMail` template is written. | Plan 08's check refuses an offered cell with no renderer. Offering a meeting invite on email with nothing behind it is the exact defect that check was extended to catch. |
| Where does it live? | `/#/meetings` (the list) and `/#/meeting/:id` (the room), with a sidebar entry under **You**. | It is a thing that is *yours*, like Profile — not something you browse. |
| Accept before joining? | **No.** Accepting says you are coming; the room is open to any participant. | A meeting you declined and then changed your mind about should not need a second round trip. Declining removes it from your list, it does not lock the door. |

## Domain model

Two hand-written tables in `app-schema.ts`, not the generated fixtures schema: a
meeting is an application concern, not part of the Product Owner's sport model,
and the generator would be the wrong owner.

```
meeting              id, title, created_by → user, starts_at (nullable), created_at
meeting_participant  meeting_id → meeting, user_id → user, status_code, responded_at
                     primary key (meeting_id, user_id)
```

`starts_at` nullable means *now* — the common case is "let's talk", and forcing a
time on it would be a form field nobody wants to fill in.

The creator is a participant with `ACCEPTED`, written at create time. That keeps
one list rather than two: "meetings I am in" is one query, and the creator does
not have to accept their own invitation.

New vocabulary, all three locales:

- `MEETING_STATUS` — `INVITED`, `ACCEPTED`, `DECLINED`
- `NOTIFICATION_CATEGORY` gains `MEETING`
- `NOTIFICATION_TYPE` gains `MEETING_INVITE`, category `MEETING`
- `ACTION` gains `CREATE_MEETING` and `RESPOND_TO_MEETING_INVITE`, both granted
  to `ANY_SIGNED_IN`
- `OBJECT_TYPE` gains `MEETING`

## API

| Procedure | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `meetings.create` | title + invitee ids → the meeting, the creator accepted, everyone else invited, and one `MEETING_INVITE` per invitee through the existing queue |
| `meetings.mine` | the meetings this reader is in, with their own status and who else is there |
| `meetings.respond` | accept or decline **your own** row, scoped to the caller like every other preference write |
| `moq.meetingRoom` | the media config for a real meeting: publish under your own id, watch every other participant |

`meetingConfig` — the dev test — is left exactly as it is.

## Notification

Sent through `notify` like every other type, so it obeys the reader's own
channel preferences without special-casing: a reader with email on for
`MEETING_INVITE` gets the mail, one with push on gets the push, one with neither
sees it in their list when they next look. That is the Product Owner's *"via
their chosen notification option"* — the existing machinery already means it, and
the only new work is the copy.

`MEETING_INVITE` is offered on both channels in `CHANNELS_FOR`, and
`tests/repo/notifications.test.ts` proves both renderers exist.

## GUI

shadcn throughout. `@shadcn/dialog` is **not installed** and is needed for the
create form; it is added with `bun run ops ui add dialog`, which is the only new
registry item.

```
Meetings
┌ Invitations ─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Standup with Niran            Niran Wongthai   [Accept] [Decline] │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌ Yours ───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Coaches catch-up              3 people · you, +2   [Open] │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                              [ New meeting ]
```

- The list is `ItemGroup` / `Item` as they ship, like every other list.
- Status is the registry `Badge`, named from the model's vocabulary.
- The create form is a `Dialog` with a `Field` for the title and the people as
  `Checkbox` rows in a `ScrollArea`-less bordered list — no new scrolling
  primitive for a first version.
- The room is the existing hang media in the page frame, with the participants
  listed beside it.

      untested is specifically `users:` with empty `targets` — and if a meeting
      invitation ever reaches the wrong person, that is the gap it came through.

## Not in this version, and said so

- **No calendar, no recurrence, no time zone picker.** `starts_at` is a nullable
  instant; scheduling is a feature, not a field.
- **No capacity limit and no lobby.** Any participant can open the room.
- **No presence.** The list does not say who is currently in the room; that
  needs the relay to be asked, which the media layer does not expose yet.
- **The people picker returns every account.** With no restrictions that is the
  instruction, but it is the one decision here worth revisiting if the platform
  ever grows past a community of clubs.
